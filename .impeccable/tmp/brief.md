# Surface brief: Varden marketing site (all routes)

Scope: Home, About, Services overview, six service pages, Insights listing and article, Contact. Mode: Persuade for marketing routes; Read for Insights articles.
Audience/job: CISO, CTO, compliance lead and scale-up founder at fintech/SaaS (US/EU) deciding whether to book a scoping call. Proof: methodology, standards, sample finding and sample report, retest included, NDA before scoping. Constraints: no invented claims, no em dashes, static export, fast and smooth.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The client's attack surface drawn as surveyed terrain; the engagement is the route across it and every cairn on the route is a verified finding. Refuses the dark navy hero with a glowing network globe and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pale stone survey sheet (#E9ECE6) with granite ink (#14181A), slate field (#2B3A42) for drenched sections, contour ink (#8FA39A) and one trail red (#E0301E) reserved for the route, the active finding and the primary action. SVG contour linework, a strict survey grid with coordinate ticks, cairn markers, finding cards set like map legends. Schibsted Grotesk display and UI, JetBrains Mono only for coordinates, IDs, endpoints and code.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own systems as terrain, sees that Varden walks the route attackers would take, understands what a finding and report look like, and books a scoping call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed survey sheet. Left five columns: h1 "Penetration testing for fintech and SaaS teams", subline, a working scoping starter (service chips, work email, Book a scoping call). Right seven columns: three parallax contour planes with a trail-red route threading four cairns into a sample finding card. Standards strip along the bottom edge.

FORM: The Survey Route, position 5 of the ordered grounded list, seed:9953a519205a314c. Raises: one strict survey grid; damped physical motion; two inks plus ground; red reserved for what matters now. Signature interaction: pinned route section where scroll draws Scope, Recon, Exploit, Report, Retest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
